Frequently asked questions

Does CareerCraft publish to LinkedIn?

No. You decide what to copy, edit and publish.

Will my photos be saved?

Uploaded images are sent for the requested analysis and are not written to your CareerCraft history. Saved history contains generated text and a short input summary.

Can AI guarantee recruiter interest?

No. The tools improve clarity and discoverability but cannot guarantee views, interviews or employment.
