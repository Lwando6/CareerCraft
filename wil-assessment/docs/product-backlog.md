## Backlog refinement rules

- The product owner reviews priority before each sprint.
- A story enters a sprint only when acceptance criteria and dependencies are clear.
- Security, privacy and data-loss defects can override feature priority.
- Estimates are comparative planning aids, not guaranteed hours.
- Status must be supported by the repository, deployment or test evidence.
