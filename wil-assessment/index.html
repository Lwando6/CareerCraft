CareerCraft Agile Project Portfolio

A traceable portfolio of the charter, user stories, product backlog, sprint planning and reflective logbook behind a South Africa-first career platform.

Project vision

One credible career profile that becomes every document a student needs to pursue opportunity.

Market
South Africa
Primary users
Students and graduates
Method
Agile and iterative

Compulsory assessment evidence

Five connected Agile artefacts

Each document links project decisions to work that can be inspected in the repository and deployment history.

01

Agile Project Charter

Vision, scope, stakeholders, success criteria, constraints and risks.

 02

User Stories and Tasks

Role-based needs, acceptance criteria and actionable implementation tasks.

 03

Product Backlog

Prioritised work, relative estimates, status and dependencies.

 04

Sprint Backlog

A focused sprint goal, selected tasks, verification and review evidence.

 05

Agile Logbook

Project periods, outcomes, lessons learned and the next improvement cycle.

Product outcome

From fragmented career information to one reusable profile

  1. BuildCapture verified career information.
  2. ShapeCreate CVs, portfolios and professional content.
  3. ApplyUse consistent, editable assets for opportunities.

Evidence standard

Clear status, honest claims

Items are marked Completed, In progress, Planned or Won't yet. The portfolio does not treat a proposed feature as delivered. Repository commits, deployment evidence and demonstrations should support the stated status.
