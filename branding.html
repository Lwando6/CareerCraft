Official Identity System

CareerCraft Brand Guidelines

A practical system for keeping every CareerCraft page, campaign, document, and social post clear, credible, and recognisably ours.

01 · Logo

Primary logo usage

Use the complete CareerCraft logo as the primary brand signature. It should feel confident and spacious, never squeezed into a crowded layout.

  • Prefer the full-colour logo on white or very light backgrounds.
  • On navy, place the full-colour logo inside a clean white container.
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the briefcase icon on every side.
  • Minimum digital width: 160px. Minimum print width: 40mm.

Do

Use approved artwork with its original proportions.

Don’t distort

Never stretch, rotate, crop, or rebuild the logo.

Don’t recolour

Avoid gradients, shadows, outlines, and unapproved colours.

02 · Colour

Core colour palette

Navy communicates trust and expertise; amber signals opportunity, momentum, and action. White and light neutrals keep the experience focused and accessible.

Career Navy

#172554

Primary headers, navigation, trust surfaces

Craft Amber

#F59E0B

Primary actions, highlights, active states

Canvas

#F9FAFB

Page backgrounds and quiet sections

Professional Ink

#1F2937

Body copy and supporting information

Recommended balance: 60% white/light neutral, 30% navy, and 10% amber. Amber is an accent—not a body-text colour.

03 · Typography

Inter, with purposeful hierarchy

Inter is the primary typeface across digital and print. Use strong weight contrast and short line lengths to keep career information easy to scan.

Hero heading

Inter Black · 36–60px · Tight leading

Section heading

Inter ExtraBold · 24–36px

Body copy should be direct, calm, and comfortable to read.

Inter Regular · 16–18px · 1.5–1.75 line height

Category label

Inter ExtraBold · 12px · Wide tracking

04 · Voice & messaging

Professional, practical, encouraging

CareerCraft speaks like a skilled career partner: confident without exaggeration, supportive without becoming vague, and ambitious without making guarantees.

Clear

Use plain language, concrete actions, and specific outcomes.

Credible

Never invent metrics, qualifications, testimonials, or employment claims.

Human

Respect each user’s starting point and explain unfamiliar career terms.

Action-led

Prefer “Build your CV” over generic phrases such as “Unlock your potential.”

05 · Imagery & icons

Real progress over stock perfection

  • People: Show authentic South African graduates, professionals, founders, and teams in credible environments.
  • Screens: Use clean product views, legible CV examples, and realistic workflow moments.
  • Icons: Use one consistent outlined or solid icon family. Icons support labels; they do not replace them.
  • Treatment: Prefer natural light, balanced contrast, and navy/amber accents. Avoid heavy filters and cliché handshake imagery.

06 · Digital experience

Interface and accessibility standards

Accessible

Meet WCAG AA contrast, label every field, and retain visible keyboard focus.

Mobile first

Use readable type, stacked layouts, and touch targets at least 44px high.

Consistent

Use rounded cards, restrained shadows, clear spacing, and one primary action per view.

Trustworthy

Explain data use near uploads, show honest errors, and never imply guaranteed hiring results.

07 · Brand checklist

Before publishing anything

✓ The approved logo is clear and correctly proportioned.

✓ Navy leads; amber highlights the most important action.

✓ Inter typography follows a clear heading hierarchy.

✓ Copy is specific, honest, and free of unsupported claims.

✓ Buttons describe the action users are about to take.

✓ Images include useful alternative text.

✓ Content works on mobile and with keyboard navigation.

✓ Personal information and uploads are handled transparently.
